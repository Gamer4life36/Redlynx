REDLYNX — a free, standalone browser


WHY WINDOWS WARNED YOU

This installer is not signed, and that is deliberate.

A code-signing certificate costs a few hundred dollars a year, and nobody
reads your code for it. It proves who wrote the software — not that the
software is safe. Signed malware exists. Unsigned honest software gets a
scary warning. I am not paying for that on free software.

"Windows protected your PC" means "we do not know who made this". It does
not mean anything was found wrong with it.


WHAT THIS BROWSER DOES NOT DO

  * No data farming. Your history, searches, bookmarks and downloads are
    files on this machine and nowhere else.

  * No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no identifiers.

  * No account, and no Redlynx server — there is nothing to send anything to.

  * No backdoor. The source is readable and every outbound connection the
    browser can make is listed in the README.

  * No updater. This is a one-time build. It will not phone home and it
    will not change under you.

Left idle, it makes no network requests at all. In normal use there is one
background connection: ad-blocking filter lists refresh from their
publishers — the same public files every ad blocker downloads, carrying
nothing about you. Settings has a button if you would rather do it by hand.


ABOUT THE ENGINE

Redlynx renders pages with Chromium, the open-source engine behind Chrome,
Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi. That is not the same thing as Chrome:
Google's layer — sync, account integration, Safe Browsing callbacks, usage
statistics — is not part of the open engine and is not in this build.

Everything above the engine is Redlynx's own: tabs, blocking, downloads,
Tor, search, reader mode. Chromium draws the page. It decides nothing about
how this browser behaves.

Chromium's sandbox and site isolation are the most heavily tested defences
in any browser, and Redlynx inherits them.


WHAT IT CANNOT PROMISE

No software can promise it is unhackable, and anyone claiming otherwise is
selling something.

Chromium has security flaws found and fixed regularly. Because this build
carries no updater, those fixes arrive only when it is rebuilt. That is the
honest price of a standalone, no-server, no-phone-home browser: nothing is
watching you, and nothing is patching you either.


WHAT IS INCLUDED

Ad and tracker blocking, a media downloader (yt-dlp, gallery-dl, ffmpeg),
the Tor daemon for .onion browsing, deep search across eleven independent
indexes, and a reader mode. All bundled, all offline, nothing to sign up for.
